Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Signal 360 handles information in connection with our automated news broadcasting and video publishing workflows.

1. Information We Access

Our application uses Google APIs (specifically the YouTube Data API v3) to automate the upload and scheduling of video broadcasts to our own designated YouTube channel. We do not access or collect personal data from viewers or visitors.

2. Use of Google User Data

Access to YouTube Data API scopes is strictly used by automated internal systems to:

  • Upload rendered MP4 news bulletins to our official YouTube channel.
  • Set video titles, descriptions, tags, and category metadata.

We do not store, transfer, sell, or share Google user data with any external parties or advertising networks.

3. Data Storage & Security

All OAuth authentication credentials and API tokens are securely encrypted and stored as CI/CD secrets within our private GitHub repository. No tokens are exposed publicly or logged.

4. Contact

For questions regarding this policy or our automated broadcasts, contact us at: storage.vignesh@gmail.com.
